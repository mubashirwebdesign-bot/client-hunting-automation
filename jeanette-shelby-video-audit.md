## Why this lead
- Voted Best Real Estate Company 12 years in a row in the Elgin Courier Poll, Best Agent in Texas 7 years, 80+ years combined experience (About page).
- Third-party WholeAustin audit scored the site 27/100, lowest in its Austin realtors list.
- Established business with reputation and budget, but a weak online front door.

## Audit — based on homepage + About page text, 19 Nov 2026 crawl
1. Hero has no clear next step in the first seconds. Homepage opens with a long paragraph about the agency, no prominent search, valuation, or book-a-viewing action in the extracted text.
2. No simple property search by price, beds, or area visible. Featured listings show as raw MLS numbers and bed/bath/sqft, with no clear filter or enquiry flow.
3. Trust is buried. 12-year Best Company award, 7-year Best Agent award, and 80+ years experience are on the About page, not in the hero where a new visitor decides to trust.
4. No lead-capture form visible in the extracted homepage text. The only contact path shown is a phone number at the bottom, 512-281-3412. No book-a-viewing or request-a-call form in the hero.
5. Spanish-speaking audience is underserved. "Se Habla Espanol" is a single line buried in the intro, with no Spanish landing page or enquiry option shown.

Top 3 for the video: 1, 2, and 3 above. Issues 4 and 5 are in the follow-up, not the video.
